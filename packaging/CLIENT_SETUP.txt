Well Product — quick start (Windows)
====================================

1. Unzip the folder you received (e.g. WellProduct-Windows.zip).
2. Open the "WellProduct" folder.
3. Double-click WellProduct.exe to start.
   The first time you run it, a "Well Product" shortcut is created on your Desktop
   (if that fails, right-click WellProduct.exe → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut)).
4. Do NOT delete other files in this folder — the app needs them all.

Your data (backup this folder)
------------------------------
After you run the app once, your database and photos are saved here:

   Desktop\WellProduct\

Typical full path:
   C:\Users\YOUR_NAME\Desktop\WellProduct\

Inside you will see:
   well_product.sqlite   — all customers, materials, products
   media\                — product photos

To back up: copy the entire Desktop\WellProduct folder to USB, OneDrive, or email (zip it).

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"
--------------------------------------------
Click "More info" → "Run anyway". This is normal for apps without a paid certificate.
